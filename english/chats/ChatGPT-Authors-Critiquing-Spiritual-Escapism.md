**2. Ludwig Feuerbach - religion as projection**

Feuerbach is extremely relevant to your “paradise in the head” idea.

> “The imagined God is loving, powerful, wise and immortal because humans desire these qualities.”

The famous book is:

\*\* *The Essence of Christianity* (1841)\*\*

He's one of the intellectual ancestors of the idea that religion is a kind of **psychological displacement**: instead of realizing human possibilities in the world, we attribute them to an imaginary supernatural realm.
